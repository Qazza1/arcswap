Frequently asked questions
When is the fee charged?
Only when a payment is executed through the ArcFXPayments smart contract — i.e. when someone pays a Pay Link or Invoice. Swaps, bridges, and history are completely free. Multisend up to 5 recipients is also free.
How is the fee collected?
Automatically on-chain. When a payment is made, the smart contract deducts 0.15% and routes it to the ArcFX treasury wallet. The recipient receives the remainder instantly. No invoicing, no manual billing.
Is there a minimum payment size?
No minimum. The 0.15% fee applies to any amount. On very small payments (under $0.67), the fee rounds to zero due to USDC's 6-decimal precision.
Does ArcFX hold my funds?
Never. ArcFX is entirely non-custodial. All transactions go directly wallet-to-wallet through smart contracts. ArcFX has no ability to freeze, pause, or access your funds at any point.
What tokens does ArcFX support?
USDC and EURC on Arc Testnet. Both are native Circle stablecoins — not wrapped versions. Mainnet support launches when Arc Mainnet goes live (Summer 2026).
When does API access launch?
The $99/month API tier is in development. Early access subscribers will be first in line. Email arcfxapp@gmail.com to join the waitlist.
