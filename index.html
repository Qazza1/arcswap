How it works
Three steps to swap

No accounts. No KYC. Just your wallet and your stablecoins.

01 — CONNECT

Connect your wallet

Connect MetaMask or any EVM wallet. ArcFX automatically switches to Arc Testnet — no manual setup.

02 — ENTER

Enter an amount

Type how much USDC or EURC you want to swap. A live rate estimate appears instantly before you commit.

03 — CONFIRM
