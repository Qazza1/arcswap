Agent Evidence

A verification surface for a decision, not a payment relay.

When an eligible invoice is analyzed, ArcFX can create an Agent Evidence bundle with the decision and its verification state. The result remains explicitly not submitted; no transaction, gas approval, or payment execution is triggered by the analysis.

Explore Agent Evidence
Evidence records the decision trail attached to an invoice. Payment authorization remains a distinct wallet-controlled boundary.
